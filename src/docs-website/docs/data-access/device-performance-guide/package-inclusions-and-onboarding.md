# 6. What's Included in Your Monitor Package

This section explains what you receive when you purchase AirQo air quality monitors: platform access, connectivity, onboarding and ongoing remote support. It also sets out which costs are covered by AirQo and which you are responsible for.

---

## Digital Platforms

Every monitor package includes access to AirQo's digital platforms. They cover the full workflow from deploying and managing sensors to analysing and sharing data.

| Module | What it does | Learn more |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Sensor management** | Register, deploy and manage your devices. Real-time monitoring and reporting on sensor performance indicators such as uptime, data completeness and battery health. | [AirQo Vertex](../../vertex/intro.md) |
| **Calibration** | Raw sensor readings are calibrated so you receive accurate PM2.5 and PM10 values. | [Data Calibration Methodology](../researchers-guide/data-calibration-methodology.md) |
| **Data access & visualisation** | Advanced data visualisation, analysis, reporting and data export. | [AirQo Nexus](../../nexus/intro.md) |
| **API access** | Programmatic access to your data, so you can build your own dashboards and integrate air quality data into your own systems. | [AirQo API](../../api/intro.md) |

---

## Connectivity (IoT SIM Cards)

Each monitor ships with an **IoT SIM card** pre-installed, so the device can send data to the platform over the cellular network.

| Item | Covered by | Details |
|------|-----------|---------|
| IoT SIM card | AirQo | Supplied with each monitor. |
| Data bundle: first 12 months | AirQo | Paid for by AirQo from the start of service. |
| Data bundle: after 12 months | Purchasing organisation | You pay for the data bundle from month 13 onward. The cost is minimal, **usually about US$1 per device per month**. The exact amount depends on the country and network operator. |

:::tip Plan for month 13
Budget for SIM data renewal before the first 12 months end. If a data bundle lapses, the device cannot transmit data, and this counts against [data availability](./data-availability-targets.md). Contact [support@airqo.net](mailto:support@airqo.net) to arrange renewal.
:::

## Onboarding & Remote Support

The monitors are designed to be easy to deploy and manage, even if your team has never used them before. Our technical team supports you remotely throughout:

- **Virtual onboarding call.** A virtual onboarding session is arranged after purchase to walk your team through the platforms and the deployment process.
- **Installation support.** Remote guidance on installing and deploying your monitors.
- **Sensor management.** Help with using the digital tools to monitor device health and performance.
- **Data access.** Guidance on retrieving, visualising and exporting your data, including through the API.
- **Follow-up calls on request.** You can ask for a follow-up call if an issue comes up. Most organisations do not need one after onboarding.

To request support or a follow-up call, email [support@airqo.net](mailto:support@airqo.net).
