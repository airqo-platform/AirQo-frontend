# Profile and Account Settings

Open **Profile** from the workspace sidebar or profile menu to manage your personal information, security, invitations, and appearance.

## Profile tab

You can update:

- First name and last name.
- Phone number.
- Job title.
- Country.
- Bio.
- Profile picture.

Email address is displayed but cannot be changed from the form. Select **Save Changes** after editing. Profile pictures can be PNG, JPG, WebP, or AVIF files up to 5 MB.

## Security tab

The Security tab lets you:

- Change your password by entering the current password, a new password, and confirmation.
- Review connected authentication providers.
- Delete your account through the account deletion flow.
- Leave the current organization when you are viewing an organization workspace and the option is available.

Account deletion is permanent. Read [Deleting Your Account](./deleting-your-account.md) before confirming.

## Team Invites tab

Pending organization invitations appear under **Team Invites**. Each invitation shows the organization, inviter, invitation date, and expiry date.

1. Open **Profile > Team Invites**.
2. Review the invitation details.
3. Select **Accept** or **Reject**.
4. Confirm the action.

Accepting an invitation may reload Nexus so that the new organization becomes available in the workspace selector.

## Theme tab

Use **Theme** to set the appearance of the Nexus interface when the theme controls are available in your environment.

## Usage tab

The **Usage** tab summarizes your personal Nexus activity and usage information
when usage data is available to your account. API plan, client, and subscription
management remain in [Console](../developer-access/console-overview.md).
